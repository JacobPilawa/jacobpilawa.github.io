Upgrade rules
  • One path can reach tier 5.
  • The second path is a crosspath and normally stops at tier 2.
  • Tier 3 and above usually define the tower's role.
  • Crosspaths commonly add range, pierce, speed, lead, or camo coverage.
Camo Bloons
  • Attacks without Camo detection cannot target Camo Bloons.
  • Common detection: Ninja, Dart Monkey 0-2-0, Sniper Monkey 0-0-1, Monkey Ace 0-2-0, Wizard Monkey 0-0-2.
  • Support detection: Monkey Village 0-2-0, Mortar Monkey Signal Flare, Monkey Sub 3-0-0.
  • Etienne gives all monkeys Camo detection at level 11.
Lead Bloons
  • Normal sharp attacks do not pop Lead.
  • Use explosions, fire, acid, or upgrades that add Lead popping.
  • Examples: Bomb Shooter, Tack Shooter 1-0-0, Wizard Monkey, Alchemist, Sniper Monkey 1-0-0.
  • Check both Lead and Camo when the rush is Camo Lead.
Purple Bloons
  • Purple Bloons are immune to magic, fire, and plasma damage.
  • Use physical attacks, explosions, blades, darts, or boomerangs.
  • Do not rely on Wizard fire, Super Monkey plasma, or other magic damage alone.
Black · White · Zebra
  • Black Bloons are immune to explosions.
  • White Bloons are immune to freezing.
  • Zebra Bloons are immune to both explosions and freezing.
  • Use a different damage type when a rush contains these properties.
Regrow · Fortified
  • Regrow Bloons can restore layers if damage is too slow or stops between hits.
  • Use continuous damage, burst damage, or a Regrow-blocking effect.
  • Fortified Bloons have extra health. Plan for more damage than the normal version.
MOAB-class
  • MOABs, BFBs, ZOMGs, DDTs, and BADs need dedicated damage or control.
  • Useful answers include MOAB Glue, MOAB Press, Maim MOAB, Icicle Impale, and high-damage abilities.
  • DDTs have Camo, Lead, and Black properties. Check all three.
Hero milestones
  • Most heroes gain their first ability at level 3.
  • Most heroes gain their main second ability at level 10.
  • Ezili and Adora have a third ability at level 7.
  • Jericho's sabotage kit starts at levels 2 and 3.
  • Level 20 is the final upgrade and usually improves the main ability.
Quick check before a rush
  • Can the defense target Camo?
  • Can it pop Lead?
  • Does the damage work on Purple, Black, or White Bloons?
  • Is there enough MOAB damage and DDT coverage?
