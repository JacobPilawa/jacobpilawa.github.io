Upgrade rules
  • One path can reach tier 5.
  • The second path is a crosspath and normally stops at tier 2.
  • Tier 3 and above usually define the tower's role.
  • Crosspaths commonly add range, pierce, speed, lead, or camo coverage.
Common checks
  • Camo detection is separate from range.
  • Lead popping is separate from damage.
  • Purple Bloons block magic, fire, and plasma damage.
  • MOAB control includes slow, stun, knockback, and damage over time.
Hero milestones
  • Most heroes gain their first ability at level 3.
  • Most heroes gain their main second ability at level 10.
  • Ezili and Adora have a third ability at level 7.
  • Jericho's sabotage kit starts at levels 2 and 3.
  • Level 20 is the final upgrade and usually improves the main ability.
